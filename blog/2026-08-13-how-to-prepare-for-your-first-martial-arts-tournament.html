Frequently Asked Questions

How can I manage pre-competition anxiety effectively?

Master Bryan Kukibo recommends practicing deep breathing exercises, visualization techniques, and focusing on the present moment to stay calm and composed.

What should I eat before a martial arts tournament for optimal performance?

A balanced meal including lean proteins, complex carbohydrates, and healthy fats, along with proper hydration, can fuel your body adequately for the competition.
